Hearts of Iron IV – Vanilla Helper Mod

A learning-focused mod for Hearts of Iron IV (Vanilla) designed to help new players understand core mechanics such as army management, production, construction, and politics.

The mod provides optional buffs and event-based tools including army enhancements, production boosts, political modifiers, commander traits, and equipment generation. All features are modular and intended for experimentation and educational gameplay.

This project is actively maintained and updated to support the latest game versions, and is included as part of my personal portfolio to showcase game modding and scripting skills.
